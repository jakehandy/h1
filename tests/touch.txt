# Velocity and pressure from the panel's keys, through fm1-base's sensing (APP_TOUCH, lib/touch.h): the
# simulator makes the readings a key would give (key K down VEL PRESS), and what h1 plays goes out over USB,
# so the note-on says the velocity it sounded at and the poly aftertouch the lean.
wait 600
out
# a soft key, a hard one, and one of the top row: a velocity from its probe and, leant on, no aftertouch
key 7 down 40
wait 100
expect_out USB 90 3C 28
key 7 up
wait 100
expect_out USB 80 3C 00
key 9 down 120
wait 100
expect_out USB 90 3E 78
key 9 up
wait 100
expect midi_out 4
key 22 down 90 60
wait 600
expect_out USB 90 4B 59
key 22 up
wait 100
# (two more out, its note-on and note-off: no aftertouch)
expect midi_out 6
out
# Leant on 60 % more than it landed, for two and a half seconds, with h1's own reading (LEAN HELD, the
# default) and then with fm1-base's (CC 118 below 64: LEAN FADES). 60 % is 76 (4C). The simulator tells the two
# apart, but backwards from what a device is expected to: it leans its pad relative to fm1-base's own rest, which
# creeps after the reading, so fm1-base's reading holds at 76 here and h1's, whose rest stays put, climbs on as
# that pad sinks under it. What this shows is that CC 118 picks a different reading, not which one plays better.
# How far a real pad drifts under a steady finger only a device can tell.
key 9 down 100 60
wait 2500
expect_out USB 90 3E 64
expect_out USB A0 3E 61
key 9 up
wait 100
# (its aftertouch back to 0 as it is let go, then the note-off)
expect_out USB A0 3E 00
expect_out USB 80 3E 00
out
midi B0 76 00
wait 50

expect_out USB A0 3E 4C
expect_no_out USB A0 3E 50
expect_no_out USB A0 3E 61
key 9 up
wait 100
expect_out USB 80 3E 00
out
